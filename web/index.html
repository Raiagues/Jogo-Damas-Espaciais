A QUICK BRIEFING

Simple moves. Plenty to think about.

01 / MOVE

Pick your flight path.

Blue goes first. Select one of your pieces, then an empty diagonal square ahead. On a keyboard, use the arrows and Enter or Space.

02 / CAPTURE

Make room for your fleet.

Jump over an adjacent opponent into an empty square. Tap the opponent or the landing square. This version allows one capture per turn; captures are optional.

03 / PROMOTE

Reach the other side.

Reach the far edge to become a king and move in either diagonal direction. Win when the other fleet has no pieces or legal moves left.

This is the project's educational ruleset: short-range kings, forward-only regular pieces, optional captures, and passing. It does not use tournament checkers rules. Once selected, a piece stays selected until a move or pass.

Damas Espaciais started as an Algorithms and Programming assignment in Aerospace Engineering at the Federal University of Santa Maria, Brazil.

The submitted C code is preserved in legacy/. The maintained edition fixes compatibility, board boundaries, and piece counting. This web interface uses the same C game engine as the desktop edition, compiled to WebAssembly, alongside the original art and sounds.
