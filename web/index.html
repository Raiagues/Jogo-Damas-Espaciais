How to play

  • Blue starts. Select a piece, then a highlighted diagonal square.
  • Jump over an opponent to capture. One capture per turn; captures are optional.
  • Reach the far edge to become a king. Kings move one square in either diagonal direction.
  • A selected piece stays selected until a move or pass. Regular pieces move and capture forward.
  • Win when the opponent has no pieces or legal moves.

Keyboard: arrow keys to navigate, Enter or Space to select.
